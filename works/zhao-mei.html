畫面以「看戲」與「辦桌」為構圖核心，拉開的布幕、碗、筷與圓桌同框，台上演的情義與台下的人情，在同一畫面共存。

本案以簡約幾何色塊為語彙延伸視覺系統；褪去繁飾，保留傳統戲曲的氣韻，讓視覺在各式製作物上有更大的彈性。

The composition centers on "watching opera" and "the banquet table" — the drawn stage curtain, bowls, chopsticks, and round table share a single frame, where the loyalty and passion performed on stage coexist with the warmth shared at the table below.

This project extends its visual language through simple geometric color blocks, stripping away ornamentation while preserving the spirit of traditional opera, giving the visual system greater flexibility across different applications.
