漸構桃園動態SHAPING TAOYUAN

活動／動態 Event／Motion

主視覺透過球體表面的紋理差異具象展覽五個核心概念「聚、構、扎、湧、鏈」。動畫延伸應用各球體符號對應的理念，使每個概念在動態中呈現出各自特色與精神；靜態視覺與動態表現相互呼應，讓視覺系統更清晰、完整地論述展覽核心。

The key visual concretizes the five core exhibition concepts — Gather, Construct, Root, Surge, Link — through distinct textural variations on the surface of spheres. Animated extensions apply the symbolic meaning of each sphere, allowing every concept to express its character and spirit in motion. Static and dynamic visuals echo one another, building a clearer and more complete visual argument around the exhibition's core ideas.
