常見到，
可能很清楚自己想說什麼，
卻不知道它應該長怎樣、對誰說。

設計師，
就是試著把這些還沒有形狀的想法，
釐清成一個能看得懂，也願意看的樣子。

有時候透過平面設計，
有時候結合動態、3D、插畫，
再不然可能得寫一點程式。

也就是，
原本需要解釋很多次的事情，
透過視覺變得更容易被理解，
讓你不用說那麼多。

It happens all the time —
you know exactly what you want to say,
but not what it should look like, or who it's really for.

A designer's job is to take those
still-shapeless ideas and work them into
something people can understand — and want to look at.

Sometimes through graphic design,
sometimes with motion, 3D, or illustration,
and sometimes that means writing a bit of code.

The point is this:
something that used to take a lot of explaining
becomes easier to grasp through visuals.
So you don't have to say as much.
