制菸所No Smoking

包裝設計 Packaging

這不是一個為了「好開」而存在的包裝，而是為了「阻礙」而生。「制菸所」是一系列反直覺的菸盒結構設計，我們利用干擾的結構、令人分心的互動，讓抽一根菸變得不再順手，或許就是那短暫的停頓與不便，進而引發使用者對吸菸議題的反思。

This packaging was not designed to open easily — it was designed to obstruct. Cigarette Obstruction is a series of counter-intuitive cigarette box structures. By incorporating disruptive mechanisms and distracting interactions, we make the act of smoking less effortless. It is in that brief pause, that small inconvenience, where reflection on the habit might begin.
