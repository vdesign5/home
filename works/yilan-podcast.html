宜蘭發聲Yilan Podcast

動態設計 Motion Design

為宜蘭在地 Podcast 節目設計一套完整的動態視覺識別系統。從節目片頭動畫、社群封面到各類數位素材，透過流動的線條與地方感十足的色彩，傳遞宜蘭的自然山海意象，讓節目在視覺上更具辨識度與地方特色。

A complete motion visual identity system was designed for a local Yilan-based podcast. From the show's intro animation to social media covers and digital assets, flowing lines and regionally resonant colors convey the natural landscape of Yilan — mountains, coastlines, and fields — giving the show a distinctive and recognizable visual presence.
