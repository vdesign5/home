品牌識別 Brand Identity

Logo以「花朵」象徵呵護與綻放，外層花瓣如同醫院溫柔的護持，承托中心的孕婦與孩子，構成一朵正在盛開生命的花。結合圓潤的曲線結構，傳達婦幼醫院的溫度與安心感，在孕育與成長的每一刻，母嬰都能被細緻照料、守護。不只醫療的承諾，是對家庭與新生命的陪伴。

The logo uses a flower to symbolize nurturing and blossoming — outer petals embracing a central form of a mother and child, blooming into a flower of life. The rounded, curvilinear structure conveys the warmth and reassurance of a women's and children's hospital, where every moment of growth is met with careful care. More than a medical promise, it is a companionship for families and new life.
